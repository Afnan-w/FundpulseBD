The Dhaka Stock Exchange (DSE) extended its recovery for a second consecutive session on 11 November 2026, with turnover climbing to a seven-week high as regulatory relief measures lifted investor sentiment.

The numbers

- The benchmark DSEX index gained 8.3 points, or 0.15%, to close at 5,445.06.
- Turnover rose 15% to Tk 757.7 crore — the highest since 23 September — up from Tk 658.5 crore the previous session.
- The blue-chip DS30 index advanced 0.10%.
- Market breadth was positive: 193 issues advanced against 153 decliners.

What drove the rally

According to Prime Bank Securities' daily commentary, two regulatory developments helped sentiment. First, the BSEC extended the deadline for brokers and merchant banks to adjust their negative equity until 2030, easing fears of an immediate capital squeeze and possible forced share sales. Second, more than 100 of the 617 frozen beneficiary owner (BO) accounts were allowed to resume trading — investors can buy, sell, and deposit fresh funds, though cash withdrawals remain blocked.

Financial institutions led sectoral gains, rising about 2%. LankaBangla Finance gained 7.52%, IPDC Finance rose 6.23% and topped the turnover chart at Tk 32.4 crore, and United Finance advanced 3.75%. Textile stocks dominated trading, accounting for Tk 180.4 crore or 23.7% of total turnover.

The bigger picture

The rally followed several weak sessions. On 9 November the DSEX had fallen to 5,392.82, and a week earlier the market's average daily turnover had dropped to around Tk 450 crore — a level the DSE Brokers Association described as unsustainable for a market that needs Tk 1,500 crore-plus in daily trading to function properly. The recovery from 10 November, when bargain hunters lifted the index by nearly 44 points, marks the strongest stretch of activity since late September.

Caveats

Analysts noted the recovery remains selective and that selling pressure continued to erode early gains in some sessions, with domestic unrest still weighing on sentiment. A few strong sessions do not confirm a trend. Daily turnover through November has remained well below the levels brokers consider healthy, and index levels are also well below their 2021 peaks.
