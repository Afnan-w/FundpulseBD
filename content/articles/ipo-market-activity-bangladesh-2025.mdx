Initial public offerings (IPOs) allow companies to raise capital by offering shares to the public for the first time and listing on a stock exchange such as the Dhaka Stock Exchange.

In Bangladesh, IPOs are regulated by BSEC. Companies must meet eligibility criteria, publish a prospectus, and comply with disclosure requirements before listing. The subscription process and allotment rules are set by BSEC and the exchanges.

Investors should read the prospectus carefully, understand the company's business and financials, and be aware that IPO shares can be volatile after listing. Applying for an IPO does not guarantee allotment, and listed prices may move above or below the issue price.

This overview is educational and does not recommend any specific IPO or company.
