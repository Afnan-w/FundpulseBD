The Bangladesh Securities and Exchange Commission has directed all listed companies to ensure timely publication of their quarterly financial reports. The directive emphasizes compliance with disclosure norms and sets penalties for delayed submissions.

Companies must publish reports within the stipulated timeframe after each quarter ends. Timely disclosure helps investors make informed decisions and supports transparency in the capital market.

Listed companies should refer to the latest BSEC circulars for exact deadlines, formats, and penalties. Investors can find published reports on company websites and through the exchanges.
