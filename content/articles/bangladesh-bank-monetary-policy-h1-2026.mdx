Bangladesh Bank's Monetary Policy Statement (MPS) for July-December 2025 (H1 FY26) outlines the central bank's policy stance amid ongoing inflation and exchange-rate pressures.

Key points from the statement:

- **Policy stance:** Bangladesh Bank continues a tight monetary policy stance in H1 FY26 to contain inflation and anchor inflation expectations.
- **Policy rates:** The policy repo rate remains unchanged at 10.0%. The Standing Lending Facility (SLF) rate remains at 11.5%, and the Standing Deposit Facility (SDF) rate remains at 8.0%. The repo rate may be adjusted downward if inflation decelerates below 7.0%.
- **Growth and inflation targets:** The statement aligns with the government's target of about 5.5% GDP growth and containing inflation within a 6.5% ceiling for FY26.
- **Exchange rate:** Bangladesh Bank adopted a more flexible exchange-rate regime in May 2025 to support stability in the foreign-exchange market and preserve reserves.
- **Financial inclusion:** The central bank promotes financial inclusion as a strategic priority, including women's economic inclusion, digital microcredit, school banking, and a cashless society. Under the Bangla QR national QR code standard, 42 banks, 7 mobile financial services (MFS) providers, and 3 payment service providers offer interoperable QR-based retail payments.

The MPS is a forward-looking policy document. Actual rates and liquidity conditions may change based on inflation trends, external sector developments, and domestic liquidity.
