The Dhaka Stock Exchange (DSE) is Bangladesh's primary stock exchange, with the DSEX as its broad benchmark index. Market activity is measured by trading volume, turnover, market capitalization, and the movement of sectoral indices.

Investor sentiment can shift with macroeconomic developments—such as inflation, interest rates, exchange rates, and policy announcements from Bangladesh Bank and BSEC—as well as corporate earnings and global market trends.

Trading statistics and top gainers/losers change daily. Readers should check the DSE website or licensed broker platforms for current prices and volumes, and should not treat daily movements as investment signals.
