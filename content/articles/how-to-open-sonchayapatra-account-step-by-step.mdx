Opening a Sonchayapatra account in Bangladesh generally follows these steps:

1. **Choose a scheme:** Decide which savings certificate fits your goal—Sanchayapatra, Pensioner Sanchaypatra, or Paribar Sanchaypatra—based on eligibility, tenure, and rates.
2. **Visit a designated branch:** Go to an NSD-designated bank branch or post office with the required documents.
3. **Submit documents and application:** Provide identification documents and complete the application form, including nomination details.
4. **Make the deposit:** Pay the investment amount as per the scheme's minimum and maximum limits.
5. **Receive the certificate:** Once processed, you will receive your savings certificate and related documents.

Encashment and redemption follow NSD rules, and procedures may vary by scheme and institution. Existing certificate holders retain their contracted rates even if new rates are later announced.

Confirm the latest document requirements, rates, and encashment timelines at your nearest NSD office or authorized bank branch before applying.
