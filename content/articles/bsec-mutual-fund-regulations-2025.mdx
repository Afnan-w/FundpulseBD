The Bangladesh Securities and Exchange Commission (BSEC) issued the Mutual Fund Regulations, 2025, which came into effect upon gazette publication on November 13, 2025. The rules introduce stricter operational, governance, and investment standards for mutual funds in Bangladesh.

Key provisions include:

- **No new closed-end funds:** BSEC will not approve any new closed-end mutual funds. Existing closed-end funds cannot extend their tenure and may be required to convert into open-end funds or face liquidation if they fail to maintain required market value.
- **Daily NAV disclosure:** Fund managers must publish the daily Net Asset Value (NAV) of open-end funds so investors can track profits or losses.
- **Capital requirements:** Asset management companies must have a minimum paid-up capital of Tk 10 crore; trustees must have at least Tk 200 crore.
- **Governance:** The chairman and chief executive officer of an asset management company cannot be the same person. Managers are barred from investing in businesses linked to their directors or affiliates.
- **Investment limits:** Fund money may only be invested in securities listed on the main board or SME platform, IPOs or rights offers of such securities, and government securities. Investment in unlisted shares, option trading, short selling, or forward transactions is prohibited. Debt or Shariah-based securities must carry a minimum credit rating of "A".
- **Reporting:** Every registered fund must submit detailed quarterly reports on portfolio holdings. Concealing price-sensitive information is treated as a punishable offence.

BSEC said the reform aims to increase investor protection, enhance transparency, and ensure accountability among asset managers and trustees.
