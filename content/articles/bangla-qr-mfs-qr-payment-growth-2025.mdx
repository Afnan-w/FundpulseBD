As part of Bangladesh's cashless society initiative, Bangladesh Bank introduced Bangla QR, a national QR code standard for low-cost, interoperable retail payments. Bangla QR allows customers to scan a single QR code to pay merchants regardless of which bank or mobile financial service they use.

As of the latest central bank reporting, 42 banks, 7 mobile financial services (MFS) providers, and 3 payment service providers (PSPs) offer the Bangla QR payment facility.

MFS and agent banking have recorded substantial growth in transactions, deposits, and lending. The total value of MFS transactions exceeded Tk 21.83 lakh crore in 2025, and MFS accounts reached about 25.02 crore. Digital microcredit facilities have also been introduced at low interest rates for underprivileged and marginalized populations to encourage digital banking habits.

QR-based payments and MFS are expanding access to digital financial services, particularly in areas where traditional bank branches are limited. Users should verify transaction fees and limits with their service provider.
