The National Savings Directorate (NSD) administers Bangladesh's savings certificate schemes, including Sanchaypatra, Pensioner Sanchaypatra, and Paribar Sanchaypatra. NSD periodically revises interest rates on these instruments in line with government policy and market conditions.

When rates are revised, the new rates generally apply to fresh purchases of savings certificates. Existing certificate holders continue to earn the rates contracted at the time of purchase until maturity or encashment, subject to the terms of each scheme.

Savers should verify the latest rates and redemption rules at their nearest NSD office or a designated bank branch before purchasing or encashing certificates. Procedures, required documents, nomination rules, and encashment timelines follow NSD guidelines.

Returns on savings certificates are government-backed, but investors should confirm current rates and terms directly with NSD or an authorized bank, as rates are subject to periodic revision.
