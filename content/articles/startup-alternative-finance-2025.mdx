Bangladesh's startup and alternative finance landscape is evolving as policymakers seek to mobilize local capital and expand access to financing for new businesses.

Bangladesh Bank's financial inclusion strategy includes digital microcredit at low interest rates for underprivileged and marginalized populations, aimed at fostering digital banking habits and entrepreneurship. School banking programs encourage early savings, and no-frills accounts bring low-income savers into the formal financial system.

At the same time, the capital market and private investors are paying more attention to growth-oriented businesses. However, the startup ecosystem in Bangladesh remains at an early stage relative to regional peers, and alternative finance options such as venture capital, crowdfunding, and angel networks are limited and largely informal.
