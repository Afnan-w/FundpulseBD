National savings certificates remain one of the most widely used government-backed savings instruments in Bangladesh. The National Savings Directorate (NSD) sets interest rates for schemes such as 3-year, 5-year pensioner, and family savings certificates.

When the NSD revises rates, the new rates generally apply to fresh purchases. Existing certificate holders continue earning their originally contracted rates until maturity or encashment, subject to scheme terms.

Savers should verify current rates at an NSD office or designated bank branch before purchasing or encashing certificates. Rates are subject to periodic government revision, and redemption procedures vary by scheme.
