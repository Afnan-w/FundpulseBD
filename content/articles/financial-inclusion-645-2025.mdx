According to Bangladesh Bank's Financial Inclusion Report 2025, about 64.5% of Bangladesh's adult population now has access to formal financial services. The report reviews developments across banking, mobile financial services (MFS), microfinance, capital markets, and insurance during 2024-2025.

Notable findings include:

- MFS transactions grew strongly, with the total value rising about 25.6% to more than Tk 21.83 lakh crore during the year. The number of MFS transactions increased by over 31% to about 9.57 billion.
- MFS accounts reached approximately 25.02 crore by end-2025, including about 10.56 crore accounts held by women.
- School banking accounts grew in number, though total deposits in those accounts declined somewhat.
- Special no-frills accounts for farmers and low-income savers saw mixed trends, with some categories recording lower deposits than the previous year.

Bangladesh Bank describes financial inclusion as a strategic priority and is working to expand access to formal financial services, particularly for underserved and vulnerable populations. Progress remains uneven across regions and demographic groups.
