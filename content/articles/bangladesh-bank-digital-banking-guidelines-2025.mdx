Bangladesh Bank has issued guidelines for digital banking transactions covering mobile banking, internet banking, and card-based payments. The guidelines aim to enhance security and consumer protection in digital financial services.

Scheduled banks are expected to implement the guidelines within the stipulated deadlines. The measures cover authentication, transaction security, customer data protection, and grievance handling for digital banking users.

As digital banking and mobile financial services grow, Bangladesh Bank continues to emphasize safe and inclusive access to formal financial services. Customers should use official banking apps and channels, keep credentials secure, and report suspicious transactions to their bank promptly.
