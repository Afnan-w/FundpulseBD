The Bangladesh Securities and Exchange Commission (BSEC) has extended the deadline for stockbrokers and merchant banks to adjust their accumulated negative equity and unrealised losses to 2030. The decision was taken at a commission meeting on 10 November 2026.

The arrangement in brief

- Institutions must make provisions against their existing negative equity — calculated as of 30 September 2026 — at a minimum rate of 25% annually from 2027.
- The existing shortfall must be fully adjusted by 2030.
- Any fresh negative equity created during 2026 beyond the 30 September level must be fully provisioned within the same year and cannot use the phased arrangement.

Background

Negative equity arises when a client's portfolio value falls below the margin loan extended against it. Years of weak market activity left many intermediaries carrying large unrealised losses. According to figures reported by The Financial Express, total negative equity stood at around Tk 110 billion as of May 2026, with provisions of roughly Tk 37 billion already held, leaving a shortfall of about Tk 73 billion.

Investor protections attached

The commission attached conditions to the extension:

- Investors with negative-equity beneficiary owner (BO) accounts may rebalance by selling shares and buying A-category shares.
- Brokers and merchant banks may not extend fresh margin loans for those purchases, and block-market transactions are barred in these accounts.
- No interest on margin loans or portfolio management fees may be charged on the affected accounts during the adjustment period.
- Investors cannot be held liable for claims or obligations arising from negative-equity accounts.
- Affected institutions may not declare or distribute cash dividends during the period.

What it means for the market

The extension relieves intermediaries of an immediate capital burden and reduces the risk of forced share sales to meet deadlines. Market commentary following the decision linked it to improved sentiment and higher turnover on the Dhaka Stock Exchange in the following sessions. Institutions that had already received extensions continue under their existing arrangements.
