Growth funds and mutual funds are both pooled investment vehicles, but they differ in structure, objective, and how investors participate.

Mutual funds collect money from many investors and invest it in a diversified portfolio of securities such as stocks, bonds, and government instruments. In Bangladesh, mutual funds are regulated by BSEC and may be open-end or closed-end.

Growth funds generally aim for capital appreciation over the long term and may have a more equity-oriented or aggressive strategy. Some growth-oriented products are structured as mutual funds or unit funds under BSEC oversight.

Key differences to consider include investment objective, risk profile, liquidity, fee structure, and whether units are traded on the stock exchange or transacted directly with the fund manager at NAV.
