Mutual fund dividends are distributions of income earned by the fund—such as stock dividends or interest on bonds—to unit holders. The dividend yield expresses annual distributions as a percentage of the fund's NAV or price.

Under BSEC's Mutual Fund Regulations, 2025, closed-end funds must distribute at least 70% of annual net profits as cash dividends, subject to trustee approval. Open-end funds follow their own dividend policies as set out in the fund's trust deed.

Dividends are not guaranteed and depend on the fund's actual earnings. Distributions may affect the fund's NAV, and dividend income may be subject to tax under Bangladesh's tax laws.

Readers should review the fund's dividend history and policy, and consult a tax professional about the treatment of mutual fund dividends.
