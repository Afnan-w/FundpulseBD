The government has kept the profit rates on all national savings certificates and schemes unchanged for the July–December 2026 half-year, according to a Finance Ministry notification issued on 2 July 2026. The rates that applied for January–June continue under the same terms and conditions, effective from 1 July.

Current rates at a glance

Rates depend on the scheme and on a two-tier structure: the first tier applies to cumulative investment up to Tk 7.5 lakh in the saver's name, the second tier to amounts above that. The figures below are the maturity rates for the first tier:

| Scheme | Term | Rate at maturity |
|---|---|---|
| 5-year Bangladesh Sanchayapatra | 5 years | 11.83% |
| Paribar (Family) Sanchayapatra | 5 years | 11.93% |
| Pensioner Sanchayapatra | 5 years | 11.98% |
| 3-monthly profit-based Sanchayapatra | 3 years | 11.82% |
| Post Office Fixed Deposit | 3 years | 11.82% |

The second-tier (above Tk 7.5 lakh) rates are slightly lower — for example, 11.80% at maturity for the 5-year Bangladesh Sanchayapatra. The 5-year schemes pay a rising rate each year (from about 9.7–9.8% in year one up to the maturity rate), while the 3-year schemes pay from about 10.6–10.7% in year one.

How the year unfolded

- 30 December 2025: The National Savings Directorate issued a notification reducing rates for January–June 2026.
- 4 January 2026: The government reversed that decision and kept the previous rates in force, following concerns from small savers.
- 2 July 2026: Rates were again left unchanged for July–December.

Things to keep in mind

- Rates apply to new purchases and to the yearly schedule for existing certificates as set by each scheme's terms.
- Source tax is deducted from profit — generally 5% for total investment up to Tk 5 lakh and 10% above that, subject to income tax rules.
- Section 48 of the Finance Act 2022 limits investment in savings instruments to Tk 5 lakh per person per fiscal year, subject to whitelisting and other conditions.
- The rates are reviewed roughly every six months, so the January–June 2027 rates may change.

Verify current rates and eligibility at an NSD office or a designated bank branch before purchasing, and compare the effective yield against bank fixed deposits after tax.
